How does the simulation work?

The simulator treats blockage as a reduction in artery diameter. It uses a simplified Poiseuille relationship to estimate relative flow.

Relative flow = (1 − blockage fraction)4 × 100%

This is an educational model, not a medical diagnostic tool. Real blood circulation is more complex.
